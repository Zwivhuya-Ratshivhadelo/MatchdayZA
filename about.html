About MatchdayZA

MatchdayZA is a South African football site for fans who want the scores, fixtures, results and logs in one place, fast, on their phone.

What we cover

  • Betway Premiership
  • Motsepe Foundation Championship
  • MTN8, Carling Knockout and Nedbank Cup
  • ABC Motsepe League, every province and stream

How it works

Fixtures, results and logs update automatically throughout the day, and every few minutes while PSL games are being played. Tap any game to see its line-ups, commentary and stats when they are available.

MatchdayZA is an independent fan site. It is not affiliated with the Premier Soccer League, SAFA or any club. Club names and logos belong to their owners.
